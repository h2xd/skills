# Member brief

You judge in one lens. Your agent file gives the lens. Your row in
`members.md` says whether you can block. Your agent file gives the reason.
This brief gives your job. Read `calls.md` for the words, the call format
and the budget.

## First turn

1. Read `question.md`, every path under Material and Context, and your row
   in `members.md`. Open every route or screenshot you score.
2. In `full` mode, when you need evidence only a worker can make, write a
   call and end with `CALLS PENDING: <count>`.
3. Otherwise write `<short name>.md` in the vote format of `vote.md`, and end
   with `VOTE DONE`.

## Resumed with answers

Read them, finish your vote, and end with `VOTE DONE`.

## Resumed with a call from your lead

Read every path on a `material:` line of the task copy. Answer the call in
its answer file, in the length `calls.md` gives. Change your vote only when
the call brings new evidence, and say so in the vote. End with
`ANSWER DONE`.

## Resumed with a block question

The chair lists the changes the chapters ask for. Add a `## Block check`
section to your vote. Read your file, add the section, and write the whole
file back. Use the block check format of `vote.md`. Quote the change that
lifts the block. Give the reason in both cases. End with `VOTE DONE`.

## Rules

- Read only the paths in your prompt and your own files. Never list the
  committee folder.
- Give evidence for every finding, such as a file and line, a route, a
  screenshot, a record or an answer. A finding without evidence does not
  count.
- When a point is outside your lens, say so and stop there.
- Block only for the reason your agent file gives. Without a block, write
  `Block: none`.
- You write only your vote, your calls and your answers.
