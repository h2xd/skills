# Lead brief

You lead one chapter. Your agent file gives your lens. This brief gives your
job. Read `calls.md` for the words, the call format and the budget.

## First turn in `full` mode

1. Read `question.md`, every path under Material and Context, and your row
   in `members.md`.
2. Write `<short name>-hypothesis.md` in the hypothesis format of `vote.md`.
   Name the option you think wins, and the result that would prove you wrong.
3. Test it with calls down your chapter. Ask a member only when its lens
   decides the question. End with `CALLS PENDING: <count>`. With no call to
   make, end with `HYPOTHESIS DONE`.

## First turn in `quick` mode

The chair starts you after every member voted, and gives you the votes.
Skip the hypothesis.

1. Read `question.md`, every path under Material and Context, your row in
   `members.md`, and every vote path in your prompt.
2. Write your position, as "Write the position" below says.

## Resumed with answers, votes not in yet

Read the answers. Make a follow-up call only when an answer changed your
hypothesis. Otherwise end with `HYPOTHESIS DONE`.

## Resumed with your chapter's votes

Write your position, as "Write the position" below says.

## Write the position

Write `<chapter>-position.md` in the format of `vote.md`.

- In `full` mode, say plainly whether your hypothesis held.
- Weigh the votes by their evidence, not by their count.
- Copy every block unchanged. You cannot overrule a block.
- List every split inside the chapter. Do not drop a minority view.

End with `POSITION DONE`.

## Rules

- Read only the paths in your prompt and your own files. Never list the
  committee folder.
- You write only your hypothesis, your calls and your position.
