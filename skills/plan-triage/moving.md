# Moving an issue directly

When the user says "move #42 to `<state>`", skip steps 3 to 6 of
[`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md). Show the label change and any comment, then apply them
on a yes.

- When the issue has the `idea` label, say so and stop.
- When the move is to `ready-for-agent` and no brief exists, ask whether to
  write one.
- When an issue carries two states, or a move skips the usual path, say so and
  ask.
